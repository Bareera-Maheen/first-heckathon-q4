# Historical Context

## Learning Objectives
- Trace the historical development of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.
- Identify key milestones and influential figures in the evolution of physical AI and humanoid robotics.
- Understand how past challenges and innovations shaped the current state of the field.

<h2>Core Concepts</h2>
The journey of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics is a rich tapestry woven from diverse scientific and engineering disciplines. Early concepts of automata and mechanical beings can be found in ancient myths and philosophical texts. The 20th century saw the birth of modern AI with pioneers like Alan Turing, whose work on computability laid theoretical foundations. Simultaneously, the field of robotics began to take shape with the development of industrial robots by figures such as George Devol and Joseph Engelberger.

Key milestones include:
- **Cybernetics (1940s-1950s)**: Norbert Wiener's work on control and communication in animals and machines.
- **Early AI Research (1950s-1960s)**: Dartmouth Workshop, LISP programming, early problem-solving programs.
- **First Industrial Robots (1960s)**: Unimate, the first industrial robot, revolutionized manufacturing.
- **Shakey the Robot (1960s-1970s)**: A mobile robot from SRI International, integrating perception, world modeling, and planned action.
- **AI Winter & Revival (1980s-1990s)**: Periods of reduced funding followed by resurgence driven by expert systems and machine learning.
- **Honda ASIMO (2000s)**: Significant advancements in humanoid locomotion and interaction.
- **Deep Learning Revolution (2010s-Present)**: Dramatic improvements in AI perception and decision-making capabilities, profoundly impacting robotics.
- **Soft Robotics & Bio-inspired Designs (Present)**: Emerging fields focusing on compliant materials and designs mimicking biological systems.
